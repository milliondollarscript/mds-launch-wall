Sell and moderate paid launch slots and featured placements for products, apps, books, events, and campaigns.

== Description ==

Launch Wall adds local-first launch boards to Million Dollar Script. Site owners can accept public launch submissions, review and approve entries, feature paid placements, and publish responsive public launch walls with clear sponsored-placement disclosure.

Provider-mode launches remain private until both administrator approval and successful payment are confirmed. Manual-mode approval confirms an offline or free placement.

WordPress personal-data tools can export matching submissions and erase submitter contact details, messages, and URLs while retaining non-identifying accounting and moderation records.

Launch Wall includes public voting, paginated boards, atomic capacity enforcement, strict submission validation, configurable abuse limits, private REST read payloads, and scheduled retention cleanup. It does not automatically expire or renew placements.
